M3 — Aradhana: live rooms, sessions and invitation services

Shanuka owns room/session presentation. This is the most backend-heavy role; deliver one verified operation at a time, not the entire engine.

Your files and boundaries

Own features/rooms/data, new live-room controllers, features/sessions/data and session controllers, plus invitation service contracts/remote implementation in features/invites/data. Agree specific filenames with Sandali before her parser work. Produce separate room/session/invite test files and feature fixture files.

Reuse the existing RoomRepository discovery contract and RoomScene inputs. RoomLobbyPreview, its local timer, RoomSeat display types and the existing room_scene_provider.dart belong to Shanuka; they are not real admission logic. Poojani coordinates shared models, memberships and rules; Shanuka owns app routes and central composition. Do not build a competing scene, auth service or router.

Done: reopening a summary/retrying leaves history count/time unchanged. Sandali can query the current user's records and clearly separate demo history. Missing records or save failures have a retry state; UI must not claim saved before success.
